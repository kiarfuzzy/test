Model Scaling Calculator

Scale a model by percentage or to hit a target dimension, and see how the weight changes along with it.

Your numbers

Any single dimension of your model — length, width, or height. Scaling applies the same factor to all axes.

Type either the scale percentage or the target dimension — the other updates.

Volume (and so weight) scales with the cube of the linear factor — a 50% scale means roughly 12.5% of the original weight.

Result

New dimension 50.00 mm
Scale factor 50.0%
New estimated weight 2.50 g
